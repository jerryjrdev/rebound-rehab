Support

Rebound Knee & Hip Rehab

Email us anytime: support@qidientlabs.com. We answer within 2 business days. Include your device model and iOS version if something misbehaves.

Getting started

  • Answer the 13 setup questions (joint, side, surgery date, mobility, goals). Rebound builds your week-by-week plan from them.
  • Your surgery date can be changed later in Today → Settings; the whole protocol re-phases automatically.
  • Pre-surgery? Choose “Still ahead of me” and Rebound switches to the prehab program.

Camera range-of-motion scanning

  • Prop your iPhone at knee height, ~2 m away, with your full leg in frame from the side.
  • Hold the bend steady for a couple of seconds; the reading locks when the angle is stable.
  • Good light and contrasting clothing improve tracking. Everything is processed on-device.
  • Camera denied or unavailable? Use Motion → Log manually — same charts, same report.

Subscription & billing

  • Plans: Rebound Pro Weekly ($13.99/week) or Rebound Pro Yearly ($129.99/year). Both unlock everything. Eligible new subscribers get a 7-day free trial; you are charged only if you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • Cancel or switch: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rebound. Cancellation stops the next renewal; access continues to the end of the paid period.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Changed phones? Tap Restore purchases on the paywall with the same Apple ID.

Your data

  • Everything is stored on-device. Export a surgeon-ready PDF from Progress → Export report.
  • To erase everything: Today → Settings → Reset all data.

Safety

Rebound is an educational companion, not medical advice. Follow your surgeon's and physical therapist's protocol first. Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain, and contact your care team about increasing swelling, redness, fever or calf pain.
