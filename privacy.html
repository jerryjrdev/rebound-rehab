Privacy Policy

Rebound Knee & Hip Rehab · Effective 18 November 2026

The short version: Rebound collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

1. Data we collect

None. Rebound does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The App Store privacy label for this app is “Data Not Collected.”

2. Data you create stays on your device

Your profile (procedure, side, surgery date), journal entries, range-of-motion readings, PT appointments, homework lists, reminders and badge progress are stored locally in the app's private container on your device. They are included in your encrypted iCloud/iTunes device backup only if you enable backups for the app, exactly like any other app data. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Camera and pose estimation

When you use camera range-of-motion scanning, video frames are processed on-device by Apple's Vision framework to estimate joint angles. Frames are never recorded, never uploaded, never sent to any server, and are discarded immediately after each measurement. Only the resulting angle (a number) is saved, and only if you choose to save it. You can deny camera access and use manual entry instead; the rest of the app works fully without the camera.

4. Notifications

Medication, icing, compression and appointment reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No push service and no server are involved.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Reports you export

The surgeon-ready PDF report is generated on-device. It leaves your phone only when you share it (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop…). Once shared, the recipient's handling of the document is outside our control — share it only with your care team.

7. Children

Rebound is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

8. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking (App Tracking Transparency is not used because there is nothing to track).

9. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

10. Contact

Rebound is published by Qidient Global Ltd. Questions about privacy or anything else: support@qidientlabs.com
